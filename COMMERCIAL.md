# Commercial licensing

Sentiniel is **source-available**, not open source. Personal and non-commercial use is free.
Commercial and enterprise use requires a paid licence from the copyright holder,
**Olamidipupo Favour**.

## What is free

- Personal use on your own machines and repositories.
- Non-commercial research, teaching, and study.
- Registered non-profits and charities, for non-commercial work.
- A 30-day evaluation before commercial use begins.

## What requires a paid licence

- Any for-profit company, partnership, or commercial entity.
- Internal business use, including internal security and incident-response teams.
- Embedding Sentiniel in a product or service that is sold, licensed, or monetised.
- Contractors and consultants using it for a commercial client.

## How to obtain a licence

Open an issue at <https://github.com/Olamidipupo-favour/sentiniel/issues> with the subject
`Commercial licence`, or contact the maintainer through
<https://github.com/Olamidipupo-favour>. Include the organisation name, expected number of users,
and whether the use is internal or part of a product. Terms and pricing are agreed in writing
before commercial use begins.
